01 — About

Attention is cheap. Being remembered is expensive. I build the second.

I'm Zeinab — a marketing strategist who treats every campaign like a small business: it has to earn its budget back. I started in a tiny Cairo social agency scheduling posts at midnight; today I lead growth across four industries and two continents.

My work sits where brand and performance meet. I write the positioning, pressure-test the creative, and then defend the numbers in the boardroom — in English, Arabic, or spreadsheet.

I believe in boring magic: weekly creative velocity, ruthless A/B discipline, and stories people actually retell. No vanity metrics, no "awareness" hand-waving — every plan ends with a number we're accountable to.

When I'm not optimizing, I'm mentoring women entering MENA growth teams and writing Al-Souq, a fortnightly newsletter on marketing craft.
